Privacy policy

MMM Uploader · Last updated 2 November 2026

Who uses this app

MMM Uploader is used only by the owner of the Mzansi Money Matters YouTube channel to upload that channel's own videos. It does not offer sign-in to anyone else.

What access it has

With the channel owner's permission, the app uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos, set their title, description, tags, privacy setting and thumbnail, on the owner's own channel only.

What data it collects

It collects no personal data from viewers or other users. The only credential it holds is the channel owner's YouTube authorisation, stored as an encrypted secret in the owner's private GitHub settings. It is not shared, sold or used for anything other than uploading the owner's videos.

Google API data

MMM Uploader's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The channel owner can remove the app's access at any time from their Google Account under Security, third-party connections.
